## To test on a device

You'll want a real Android 11+ device (the emulator's `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` is unreliable):

1. Pack a speech bundle with `group: "speech"`, `unpack_phase: "on-demand"` in `config.json`.
2. Push the `.dat` to `/sdcard/룡마/가상외국어회화/data/`.
3. Launch the app, sign in, tap a scenario → you should see the speech-setup screen unpack it with a progress bar, then let you into the conversation.

One real-device caveat worth knowing: the first-launch `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` permission grant — if the app can't read `/sdcard/룡마/...` yet, the installer falls back to the app-scoped external dir, so drop the `.dat` wherever the `[datapack] paths:` log line reports.
